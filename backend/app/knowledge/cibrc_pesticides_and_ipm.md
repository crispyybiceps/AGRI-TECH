# CIB&RC Approved Pesticides, Dosages, PHI, and Safety Precautions

## 1. Strictly Banned Pesticides in India
The following chemicals are strictly prohibited under the Insecticides Act, 1968 and CIB&RC orders:
- **Monocrotophos**: Banned on vegetables and sugarcane. High toxicity.
- **Endosulfan**: Completely banned across India by Supreme Court order.
- **Paraquat Dichloride**: Restricted/banned in multiple states; never recommend for smallholder spraying.
- **Carbofuran 3G / Phorate 10G**: Severe mammalian toxicity; avoid in vegetable and open field crops.

## 2. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) First Policy
Always recommend cultural and biological controls before chemical application:
- **Pheromone Traps**: Install 5 traps per acre for Pink Bollworm (Cotton) and Fall Armyworm (Maize).
- **Yellow / Blue Sticky Traps**: Install 10-15 traps per acre for sucking pests (aphids, jassids, thrips, whitefly).
- **Neem Formulations (Azadirachtin 10,000 ppm)**: Dosage: 2.0 to 2.5 ml per liter of water. Apply at early pest infestation stage.
- **Trichoderma viride / harzianum**: 5 g per kg of seed for seed treatment against root rot, wilt, and damping-off.
- **Beauveria bassiana (1.15% WP)**: 5 g per liter of water for eco-friendly fungal control of caterpillars and whiteflies.

## 3. Approved Chemical Insecticides (CIB&RC Registered)
When pest population exceeds Economic Threshold Level (ETL):
- **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen)**:
  - Target: Stem borer, fruit borer, diamondback moth, pod borer.
  - Recommended Dosage: 0.3 ml to 0.4 ml per liter of water (approx 60 ml per acre in 150-200 L water).
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 14 days on soybean, 20 days on pigeonpea, 3 days on tomato.
- **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG**:
  - Target: Bollworms in cotton, pod borers in pulses, fruit borer in tomato.
  - Recommended Dosage: 0.4 g to 0.5 g per liter of water (80-100 g per acre).
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 5 days on vegetables, 10 days on cotton.
- **Thiamethoxam 25% WG**:
  - Target: Sucking pests (jassids, aphids, whitefly in cotton, soybean).
  - Recommended Dosage: 0.3 g to 0.5 g per liter of water (80 g per acre).
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 21 days.

## 4. Mandatory Safety Precautions
Every chemical pesticide recommendation MUST state:
1. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: Wear rubber gloves, safety face mask, goggles, and full-sleeved shirt while mixing and spraying.
2. **Timing**: Spray only during cool hours (early morning 7-10 AM or late afternoon 4-6 PM). Never spray against wind direction or during high wind (>15 km/h).
3. **Storage & Child Safety**: Keep chemicals locked in original containers, completely out of reach of children and farm animals. Never store near food grain or water sources.
4. **Disposal**: Triple rinse empty bottles, puncture them, and bury safely away from water bodies. Never reuse pesticide containers for household water or food storage.
5. **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI)**: Do not harvest crops before the specified waiting days have elapsed after the last spray.
