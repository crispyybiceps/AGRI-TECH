# Maharashtra Crop Selection and Agronomic Suitability Guide

## 1. Agro-Climatic Zones of Maharashtra
- **Western Maharashtra (Pune, Satara, Solapur, Kolhapur, Sangli)**:
  - Soils: Deep black cotton soils (Vertisols) in river valleys; light soils in rain-shadow areas.
  - Recommended Crops: Sugarcane (assured irrigation), Soybean (JS-335, Phule Kalyani), Rabi Sorghum (Maldandi M-35-1), Pomegranate, Grapes, Onion.
- **Marathwada (Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar, Jalna, Beed, Latur, Nanded, Parbhani)**:
  - Climate: Semi-arid, dependent on south-west monsoon (600-800 mm).
  - Recommended Crops: Soybean (JS-9305, JS-335), Cotton (Bt hybrids like Ajit-155), Pigeonpea (BDN-711, BSMR-736), Rabi Jowar (Phule Suchitra), Bengal Gram / Harbhara (Digvijay, JAKI-9218).
- **Vidarbha (Nagpur, Amravati, Wardha, Yavatmal, Akola, Buldhana)**:
  - Soils: Heavy black clay soils with high water holding capacity.
  - Recommended Crops: Cotton, Soybean, Pigeonpea, Citrus (Nagpur Mandarin Orange).
- **Khandesh (Jalgaon, Dhule, Nandurbar)**:
  - Recommended Crops: Banana (Grand Naine), Cotton, Maize, Groundnut.
- **Konkan (Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg, Raigad, Thane)**:
  - High rainfall coastal zone (>2500 mm). Lateritic and alluvial soils.
  - Recommended Crops: Paddy / Rice (Indrayani, Karjat-3), Alphonso Mango, Cashew, Coconut.

## 2. Crop Rotation Best Practices
- **Soybean followed by Gram (Harbhara)**: Excellent nitrogen-fixing rotation. Gram utilizes residual moisture from Kharif.
- **Cotton intercropped with Pigeonpea (1:1 or 2:1)**: Provides pest buffer and spreads economic risk against drought.
- **Sugarcane followed by Green Manuring (Dhaincha/Sunhemp)**: Restores soil microbial biomass and reduces soil compaction.
