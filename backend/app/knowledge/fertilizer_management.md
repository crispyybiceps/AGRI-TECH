# Integrated Fertilizer Management and Soil Nutrient Guidelines

## 1. Balanced NPK Nutrition (4:2:1 Ratio Standard)
- **Nitrogen (N)**: Essential for vegetative leaf growth and green canopy. Excess N causes lodging, delays maturity, and attracts aphids/sucking pests.
- **Phosphorus (P)**: Crucial for early root development, nodulation in pulses, and flowering. Must be placed 5 cm below seed level as P is immobile in soil.
- **Potassium (K)**: Increases plant water use efficiency, strengthens cell walls, improves disease resistance, and aids grain filling.

## 2. Soil pH Impact on Nutrient Availability
- **pH 6.5 - 7.5 (Optimal)**: All macro and micronutrients are most soluble and available to plant roots.
- **pH < 6.0 (Acidic)**: Phosphorus binds with iron and aluminum; calcium and magnesium become deficient. Apply agricultural limestone.
- **pH > 8.0 (Alkaline/Calcareous)**: Phosphorus binds with calcium as insoluble calcium phosphate; zinc and iron become unavailable (causing leaf chlorosis). Apply zinc sulfate (10 kg/acre) and elemental sulphur.

## 3. Recommended Fertilizer Doses for Major Maharashtra Crops
- **Soybean (per acre)**: 10 kg N + 24 kg P2O5 + 12 kg K2O + 8 kg Sulphur. Apply entire P and K at sowing; apply Rhizobium seed treatment.
- **Bt Cotton (per acre)**: 40-50 kg N + 20-25 kg P2O5 + 20-25 kg K2O. Split Nitrogen into 3 doses: 20% basal, 40% at square formation, 40% at boll development.
- **Sugarcane (per acre)**: 100 kg N + 45 kg P2O5 + 45 kg K2O in 4 splits during earthing-up operations.
