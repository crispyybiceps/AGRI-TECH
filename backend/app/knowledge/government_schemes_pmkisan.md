# Key Government Agricultural Schemes for Maharashtra Farmers

## 1. Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- **Benefit**: Financial benefit of Rs 6,000 per year paid in 3 equal installments of Rs 2,000 directly via DBT into bank accounts.
- **Eligibility**: All landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their names.
- **Mandatory Requirements**: Aadhaar e-KYC, land record seeding (Bhulekh / 7/12 extract linked with Aadhaar), NPCI active bank account.

## 2. Namo Shetkari Maha Samman Nidhi Yojana (Maharashtra State Scheme)
- **Benefit**: Additional Rs 6,000 per year from Government of Maharashtra paid in 3 installments of Rs 2,000 to beneficiaries of PM-KISAN.
- **Total Combined Income Support**: PM-KISAN (Rs 6,000) + Namo Shetkari (Rs 6,000) = Rs 12,000 per eligible farmer family per year.

## 3. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY / Rs 1 Crop Insurance)
- **Special Feature in Maharashtra**: Farmers pay only Rs 1 per application form to insure notified food crops, oilseeds, and annual horticultural crops against non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, unseasonal rain, hailstorms, pest outbreaks).
- **Post-Harvest Loss**: Coverage available up to 14 days after harvest for crops kept in field for drying.
- **Claim Intimation**: Farmer must inform the insurance company or toll-free helpline (14447) within 72 hours of localized calamity.

## 4. MahaDBT Farmer Schemes Portal
- **Subsidies**: Subsidies up to 50-80% on drip irrigation systems, sprinkler sets, farm tractors, solar water pumps (PM-KUSUM / Mukhyamantri Saur Krushi Vahini), and farm ponds (Magel Tyala Shet-Tale).
- **Application Portal**: Apply online at mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in using Aadhaar number and 7/12 land extract.
