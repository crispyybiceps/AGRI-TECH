# Soil Health Card Interpretation and Problem Soil Reclamation

## 1. Soil Health Card 12 Parameters
Official Government of India Soil Health Cards evaluate 12 essential parameters:
1. Macro-nutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K).
2. Secondary nutrient: Sulphur (S).
3. Micro-nutrients: Zinc (Zn), Iron (Fe), Copper (Cu), Manganese (Mn), Boron (B).
4. Physical/Chemical parameters: pH (Reaction), EC (Electrical Conductivity / Salinity), OC (Organic Carbon).

## 2. Problem Soils Reclamation in Maharashtra
- **Saline Soils (EC > 2.0 dS/m, pH < 8.5)**:
  - Cause: Poor drainage, high water table, excessive irrigation with borewell saline water in canal command areas (Solapur, Kolhapur, Sangli, Pune).
  - Remediation: Install sub-surface tile or open drainage ditches (depth 1 to 1.2 m). Flush with fresh canal water to leach out dissolved chlorides and sulphates.
- **Sodic / Chopan Soils (pH > 8.5, ESP > 15%)**:
  - Cause: Excess sodium adsorbed on clay particles, causing soil dispersion, crusting, and impermeability.
  - Remediation: Apply Agricultural Gypsum (Calcium Sulphate) at 500 kg to 1 tonne per acre. Calcium displaces sodium from clay colloids. Follow with flushing irrigation and grow green manure (Dhaincha).
- **Acidic Soils (pH < 6.5)**:
  - Cause: High rainfall leaching basic cations in Konkan coastal belt (Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg).
  - Remediation: Apply agricultural lime (calcium carbonate) or dolomite at 250-500 kg per acre every 3 years.
